Privacy

RATEL RAGE by KenCrafts Interactive Studio · last updated 12 November 2026

You can play RATEL RAGE without giving us any personal details. We collect a little anonymous gameplay data to see how many play, where it is too hard and what breaks. If you choose to make an account, or to advertise on the billboards, we keep the details you give us for that, and nothing more. We never sell your data, and there is no advertising tracking.

1. Anonymous gameplay stats (everyone)

  • Gameplay events: session start, a one-minute "still playing" signal while on screen, level start, Darki knocked out (level and arena section), retry, quit, and level finish (time, score, rank).
  • Device type, not identity: mobile / tablet / desktop, browser and OS (with versions), language, screen size, and a rough memory / CPU class.
  • Performance and errors: load time, frames per second, and error text (message and file name only).
  • Country: the two-letter code our host (Cloudflare) attaches to each request. Nothing more precise.
  • Random IDs: one per tab session, plus a random player ID in your browser's storage so returning players count once. Not linked to who you are. With "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" on, no player ID is stored.

These stats do not include your name, email, phone number, contacts, precise location or IP address. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts page visits, without cookies and without identifying visitors.

2. Your game progress and account (optional)

  • As a guest (the default), the game gives you an anonymous account so your progress can be saved online: best scores, levels cleared, the furthest section and your last checkpoint, plus a random display name. No email is asked for. Your progress is also kept on your device.
  • If you register, we also keep your email address and a password (stored only in scrambled, hashed form). We use your email to confirm your account and to reset your password, and for nothing else: no newsletters or marketing.
  • The leaderboard (registered players only) shows your display name, score, rank, time and date to other players. You choose the display name; it never shows your email.
  • Gameplay events from a signed-in player are also stored against their account ID, so progress and the leaderboard can be checked.
  • Security: our account provider sees the IP address you connect from, to stop abuse such as mass sign-ups, and keeps it in its security logs. We do not put it in our game data.

Accounts run on Supabase (servers in Ireland, EU). Account emails are sent through Resend (USA) from no-reply@ratelrage.com.

Under 18? You can play as a guest without giving any personal details. Please ask a parent or guardian before registering with an email address.

3. Billboards and advertisers

Players: some in-game billboards show paid adverts, fetched from our ad server. For advertisers we count, per board per day, how often a board was on screen and how often its link was opened from the pause menu. Totals only: no ID, account, device details or anything about you is sent. Opening an advert's link takes you to the advertiser's own website, which has its own privacy policy.

Advertisers: when you book a billboard we keep your business and brand name, contact name, email, phone number (if given), your tap link, any note, your artwork, your dates and price, the policy version you agreed to, your payment and refund records, and your advert's view and tap totals. We use these to review, run, invoice and report on your booking and to handle cancellations and refunds.

  • Payments: online payments are handled by Paystack. Your card or bank details go to Paystack, not to us; we only receive a payment reference and the amount. Refunds go back the same way, or by bank transfer if you paid by invoice.
  • Your booking's status page is private: only people with its link can open it.

4. Where it is kept, and for how long

  • Gameplay stats and advert bookings: on Cloudflare's servers. Gameplay events are deleted after 180 days and session summaries after 400 days.
  • Accounts and online saves: on Supabase, for as long as the account exists. Delete your account (see below) and its profile, saves, leaderboard entries and events are deleted with it.
  • Advertiser records: for as long as the booking runs and then for as long as we need them for our tax and accounting records.

Some of these providers are outside Nigeria. They process the data only on our behalf. Only the developer can read it. It is used to run and improve the game and the billboards, and is never sold or shared for anyone else's marketing.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Clearing this site's browser data deletes the anonymous player ID; "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" stops it being created.
  • You can play without an account, change your display name at any time, and sign out.
  • Under Nigeria's Data Protection Act you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted (including your whole account), object to how we use it, or take a copy of it. You can also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

For any of these, or other privacy questions, contact KenCrafts Interactive Studio.
