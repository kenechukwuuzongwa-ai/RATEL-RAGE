Description

Short: Ratel Rage is a 2D arcade beat-'em-up on the streets of Lagos. Fight the Agberos collecting an illegal street tax, and follow the money up to the people behind them.

Long: You are Darki, a street fighter who won't pay to stand his ground. On Balogun Street, MC Olodo's enforcers tax every trader and bus, and the money flows to a network nobody can name. Brawl with jabs, kicks, grabs, throws, a 5-hit combo, a shoulder-charging RamRage and the street's own bats and machetes. Fill your Rage, finish bosses with timed presses, and piece together the evidence that points to the next name.
