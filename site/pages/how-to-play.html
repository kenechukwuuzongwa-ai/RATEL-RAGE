Basics

Move

Left and right, plus toward and away from the camera. Enemies come from both sides, so keep moving.

Jump

Attack in the air for an air kick. Jump out of a rush for a running leap.

Pause

Resume, restart from the last checkpoint, options, or quit. Your controls layout is under Options.

Your touch buttons

The left half of the screen is the movement stick: put your thumb down anywhere and drag. Every button can be moved, resized and faded in Options › Controls › Customize controls, with three saved layouts.

Controller layout

Plug in or pair any standard controller and press a button. Face buttons: bottom jumps, left jabs, right kicks, top uppercuts. Left shoulder blocks, right shoulder throws the 5-hit combo. Left trigger picks up, carries and throws, and starts finishers. Right trigger grabs for the beat-down. Start pauses. Rumble can be turned off in Options › Gameplay › Vibration.

Attacks

Jab

Quick and hard to punish. Chains into any other move.

Power

5-hit combo

Four punches and a kick that can't be interrupted. Land all five on a boss and it opens his finisher.

Power

High kick

Knocks the man down.

Power

Back kick

Hits the man behind you without turning. Press the direction and the kick together.

Power

Uppercut

Heavy, knocks down, and cuts into a move that hasn't committed yet.

Power

Air kick

Once per jump. Knocks down and sends men further. From a running leap it hits harder still.

Grab and throw

Pick up and throw

Lift the man in front of you. Hold to carry him (slower, no attacks). Let go to throw. Hold longer before letting go and the throw is up to twice as strong. The body knocks down whoever it hits.

Power

Beat-down

Pull him in, six quick strikes, then a grab uppercut. Press JAB within a fifth of a second of GRAB.

Power

Fist combo

Punch again early in the beat-down and it becomes the long version: twelve more punches first. Mashing doesn't make it longer.

Power

Pressing a direction during a grab breaks out of it and turns you that way, so you are never stuck when someone comes from behind. Bosses can't be lifted, and can only be grabbed once every 4 seconds.

Rush and RamRage

Rush

With a man in range, Darki charges straight at him and stops just short. With nobody there, it is a sprint. Rushing uses the stamina gauge.

Rush follow-ups

Within a second of a rush: JAB pulls him into the beat-down, KICK is a flying rush kick that sends him into the man behind, a double-tap is the 5-hit combo.

RamRage

A shoulder charge that ploughs through standing, airborne and fallen men. Needs a full stamina gauge and empty hands. Nothing can stop it once it starts. During Rage it is free.

Power

The stamina gauge

Starting a rush costs a slice of it and running drains it. It refills after a short pause. Run it to empty and Darki is winded: the refill waits longer. A full gauge is about a second and a half of running. During Rage it never runs out.

Rage

The bar fills as you land hits and as you take them. Hits you take can fill it, but never set it off: the burst waits for your next landed blow.

  • A shockwave in front of you knocks men down.
  • For 7 seconds every hit does 1.6× damage.
  • You move 1.3× faster and the stamina gauge never empties.
  • RamRage costs nothing, and with the machete you can spin the Rage Tornado.

Damage dealt in Rage counts at its full raged value in your score.

Block and health

Block

Blocked hits from the front do no damage. Hits from behind still land. The Agbero side kick breaks your guard for half a second. You can't block in the air or while carrying.

Recovering

Stay out of the fight for 5 seconds (no hits landed or taken, nobody close) and health starts coming back. The bar says + RECOVERING.

Medpacks

One drops on your 6th and 15th knockout of a level and when the first of the boss's crew falls. Each heals 35% of your health, and is only picked up when you are hurt.

One life

If Darki goes down, it is game over. RETRY starts you from the last checkpoint: each street gate, the boss, and the Level 02 strong room.

Boss finishers

When the boss is worn down to half health, stand close in front of him in his lane and press GRAB. A finisher starts and rings appear: press on each one as it closes.

  • PERFECT adds 30% damage, GOOD 15%, a miss nothing.
  • The last blow lands at ×0.7 (failed) up to ×1.4 (perfect).
  • Landing a full 5-hit combo on him also opens a finisher.

One finisher doesn't end him. It takes several, or good old punching in between.

Street rules

Gates

The street is split into stretches. The way forward opens when a wave is cleared.

No running away

You can go back at most one cleared stretch. Further back is closed, so camping to heal doesn't work.

Weapons

One at a time. You drop it when knocked down. See weapons.
