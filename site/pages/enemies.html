Know the street

Enemies and bosses

Four kinds of men stand between Darki and the people they work for.

Level 01

Agbero

The street enforcers. Quick jabs, and a side kick that breaks your block and leaves you open for half a second.

Beat them: don't sit in your guard against an Agbero. Jab first, or grab him and throw him into the others.

Level 01

Taskforce

The heavier men of the crew, first seen in wave 2.

Beat them: they need a strong throw to go over, so charge it before letting go. Uppercuts and the 5-hit combo work well.

Level 01

Aye

Carries a machete. Knock him down and he drops it, then goes back to pick a blade up.

Beat them: put him down, then take the machete yourself with GRAB before he gets back to it.

Level 02

SARS

Officers who throw knives from range.

Beat them: close the distance with a rush. You can't use their knives.

Boss · Level 01

MC Olodo

The Motorpark Chairman fights with his crew around him. Straight punches, a heavy cross, a spinning hook kick and a spinning flurry. Below about half health he gets angry (the bar reads VEX!) and faster.

Beat him: clear his crew when you can, keep blocking his heavy hits, and use the finisher once he is down to half. A full 5-hit combo on him opens one too.
