Reading the numbers

Views a day

How many times a day, on average, a player passed the board with at least half of it on screen for at least one second. Each pass counts once.

Seconds on screen

How long each view kept the board on screen, on average. Boards where players stop to fight are on screen longer than boards they run past: more time, more chance your brand is noticed.

Views that tapped

The share of views where a player opened your link from the pause menu's VISIT POSTS.

Where it stands

The stretch of the street and what is happening in front of the board: which fight, which pickup, which boss.

No personal data is collected for these numbers: only totals per board per day. Views and taps aren't promised; they are what players actually did.

How booking works

Pick a board

Each board is shared in rotation by up to its number of shares. Book every share and it is yours alone.

Upload your artwork

Fit, zoom and place it while you watch it on the real board. A template is there if you need it.

Choose dates

A start date and 1 to 12 months. Add your business details and the link players will open.

Review and pay

We check it against the policy. Once approved and paid, it goes live within minutes, and your private status page shows views and taps.

In the game

Your advert replaces the board's face, lit like the street. It never changes while a player is looking: rotation only moves on when the board is fully off screen. In the pause menu, VISIT POSTS shows every advert on the player's stretch with a button to your link.

Who sees it

Ratel Rage is for everyone and runs in the browser on phones and computers. Everyone who plays Level 01 walks this street.

The rules in short

Welcome

Food and drink, shops and fashion, phones and telecoms, apps and games, music, films and events, schools and courses, local services, sport, banks and licensed payment services, charities that don't campaign politically.

Betting and alcohol

Allowed with conditions: a licensed operator (licence number on your link page), "18+" and a responsible-play or "Drink responsibly" message, nothing aimed at children, no promised wins.

Not allowed

Tobacco, vapes and drugs; sexual content and dating; weapons; get-rich-quick schemes; politics; health claims; hate and shock images; scams; other people's brands; anything illegal; artwork that pretends to be part of the game.

Questions

Do I need to play the game?

No. Everything is on this page and the booking site: where each board stands, what players are doing there, the live numbers and the price.

How do refunds work?

Fixed terms: everything back if it isn't approved or you cancel before it starts; the unused days back if you cancel after; nothing back if it is taken down for breaking the policy. Full terms are in the policy.

Can I change my artwork?

Artwork is fixed per booking, because each one is reviewed. For new artwork, make a new booking.

Are there boards in Level 02?

Not yet. All bookable boards are on Level 01's street.
