Off the street

Weapons

One weapon at a time. Get knocked down and you drop it. A dropped weapon lies on the ground for 12 seconds.

Show controls for

Taking and dropping

Pick up

Walking into a weapon never picks it up: press GRAB at it. A GRAB button appears over it when you are in reach.

Throw it

The same button throws what you are holding. A thrown bat hits up to 2 men, a thrown machete cuts through up to 3.

Where

Bat: floating just past the first gate of Level 01 (it comes back if no bat is left anywhere). Machete: dropped by a knocked-down Aye. Level 02: two bat racks and two machete racks in the strong room.
