Operations

Levels and difficulty

Two playable operations today, a third on the way.

Level 01 · Lagos Island · Balogun Street

The Street Tax

  • Three street waves of Agberos, Taskforce and machete-carrying Aye, each behind a gate.
  • A floating bat just past the first gate (press GRAB at it).
  • The boss: MC Olodo and his crew. He gets faster when he is badly hurt.
  • After the boss: the evidence board.

Objectives: end Olodo's street tolls, beat his Agbero enforcers, bring him down.

Level 02 · Lagos · NPF Evidence & Property Wing

Operation Clean Sweep

  • Six stretches through the facility, with SARS officers throwing knives.
  • The strong room: crack the keypad, reach the laptop, then defend the server while it copies. Waves come through the door. If the server falls, the evidence is lost.
  • Bat and machete racks in the strong room.

Unlocks when you finish Level 01.
