Scoring and grades

Your grade is your total score for the level, put into bands. Nothing else decides it.

The grades

S32,000+A near-perfect run: fast, long combos, little damage taken.
A25,000+Strong and quick.
B18,000+A solid clear.
C11,000+You made it through.
Dbelow 11,000Room to grow.

The results screen shows the key too, and how many points you needed for the next grade.

Where the points come from

PartPointsNotes
Knockouts150 eachEvery enemy you put down.
Combatdamage × combo multiplierEvery point of damage you deal, raised 0.25× per 10 hits in the streak (up to 3×). Rage damage counts at its raged value. A thrown body hitting another man counts; a body hitting a wall or a vehicle doesn't. Clutch saves add their bonus here.
Best combo50 per hitYour longest streak in the level.
Top hit5 per pointYour single biggest blow.
Time40 per second under 600Clear the level in under 10 minutes and every second saved scores. A 4-and-a-half minute clear is worth about 13,000.
Healthup to 2,000The share of your health left at the end.

The score at the top of the screen during a fight is knockouts, combat, best combo and top hit. Time and health are added on the results screen.

How to raise it

Go fast

Time is the biggest lever: a quick clear can be worth more than everything else together.

Don't get hit

A hit taken breaks your streak and its multiplier, and costs health points at the end. Block, and keep men in front of you.

Save the counter

Land a hit as the counter starts falling apart. Clutch saves stack up to 4.

Use Rage on crowds

Rage damage is 1.6×. Save the burst for a full wave or the boss.

Throw bodies

A charged throw into a group is damage on several men at once.
