Chains

Starter

5-hit combo

Two taps inside about a third of a second. The second tap cuts the first jab short and runs four punches and a finishing kick. It can't be interrupted, so start it when the man is already in front of you.

Starter

Beat-down

GRAB, then JAB within about a fifth of a second. If you are too slow, the GRAB becomes a pick-up instead.

Skilled

Fist combo (long beat-down)

A second punch early in the beat-down (in the first half second) locks in the long version: four rounds of three punches before the six strikes and the uppercut. Its length is fixed, so there is no need to mash.

Skilled

Rush into a beat-down

Rush at a man, then JAB in the window after you arrive. The quickest way to grab someone across the street.

Skilled

Rush into the 5-hit combo

The same window takes a double-tap: you arrive already throwing the combo.

Skilled

Rush kick

A flying kick out of any rush. The man you hit is sent into the man behind him.

Skilled

Running leap

Jump during a rush and you keep most of the speed. An air kick from a running leap hits harder: the faster you were going, the more it does.

Heavy

RamRage

A short tap back, then forward straight after. The stamina gauge must be full (it costs most of it). It hits men standing, in the air and on the ground, and it won't stop for anything. Free during Rage.

Heavy

Bowling

Pick a man up, hold to charge, let go. The body knocks down whoever it hits, and damage from bodies hitting other men counts in your score.

Heavy

Rage Tornado

Machete in hand, during Rage (or with a full Rage bar). Hold the second press: Darki spins, hitting everyone around him, and you can steer it. It lasts while you hold and Rage lasts.

Keep the hit counter alive

The streak

Each landed hit adds to the counter. It breaks if you stop hitting for too long (the window gets shorter as the streak grows) or the moment you take damage.

The multiplier

Every 10 hits in a streak, the damage you deal scores 0.25× more, up to 3×. Callouts at 10 BRUTAL!, 20 SAVAGE!, 35 UNSTOPPABLE! and 50 RATEL RAGE!

Clutch

When the counter starts falling apart, land a hit before it is gone and you save the streak. Each save adds 25 points to every later hit in that streak, up to 4 saves.
