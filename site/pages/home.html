Streets of Justice

No receipt. No mercy. No more street tax.

Ratel Rage is a 2D arcade beat-'em-up set on the streets of Lagos. You are Darki. The Agberos are collecting "tolls" from every trader and bus, and the money runs uphill to people nobody can name. Fight your way to them.

New to the fight?

The basics in 60 seconds

Pick how you play. The buttons below change to match.

Move

Walk left, right, toward the camera and away from it. Enemies come from both sides.

Jab

Fast and safe. Tap twice quickly for the 5-hit combo.

Kick

Heavier, and it knocks men down. Push away from the way you face for a back kick.

Block

Hits from the front do no damage while you hold it. Hits from behind still land.

Grab

Lift a man, carry him, and throw him into the others. Press JAB right after GRAB for a beat-down instead.

What your grade means

Your grade is your total score for the level. 32,000 or more is an S. Knockouts, damage, long combos, a fast clear and the health you finish with all add to it.
