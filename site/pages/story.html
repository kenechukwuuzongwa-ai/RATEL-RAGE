Level 01 · The Street Tax · Case file 001

MC Olodo, Motorpark Chairman

MC Olodo controls the streets through fear and intimidation. As Chairman of one of Lagos's most powerful motorparks, he collects illegal tolls from transporters, traders and small businesses.

His Agbero enforcers keep the money flowing, and during elections they become muscle for the politicians who protect him.

But Olodo is not the architect. He is the collector. His operation feeds directly into a network known only as The Cabal.

Every empire has a first brick.

What the case uncovers

Olodo falls, and his phone talks: the recordings on it point past the motorpark to a senior police officer, AIG Moshun Jimoh.

Level 02 · Operation Clean Sweep · Case file 002

AIG Moshun Jimoh

Photo hidden
(spoiler)

Olodo's operation leads into the police itself, and into the evidence wing where a murder case's files are kept. Turn spoilers on to read the dossier.

Senior police officer. Powerful connections. Untouchable reputation. Intelligence suggests his real power doesn't come from his badge. It comes from the people behind it: politicians, contractors, street enforcers, criminal networks.

For the right price, investigations disappear, witnesses change their stories, and evidence never makes it to court. His name surfaced in the aftermath of the Ahmad Akande murder case.

Two men are dead, one man stands accused, and someone with considerable influence is determined to make sure the truth never comes out.

Darki breaks into the NPF Evidence & Property Wing, fights through Jimoh's men, and copies the server while they try to take it back.

What the case uncovers

The copied server shows whose office signs off Jimoh's payments: a serving federal minister.
