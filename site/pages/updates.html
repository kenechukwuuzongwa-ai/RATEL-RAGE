What's new

Updates

The game updates itself: reload, or reopen the app, to get the latest.

November 2026 · version 170

  • Keyboard players can now do everything: I kicks (with the direction held away, a back kick), and J twice quickly throws the 5-hit combo and drives the grab's fist combo.
  • Touch: holding KICK and tapping JAB now gives a clean uppercut, without a kick first.
  • Four options that did nothing yet were removed from the menu.
  • This website, with a full move list, combos, and what the grades mean.

November 2026

  • iPhone: the game fills the screen in landscape, with the touch buttons clear of the notch, and no longer fails to load on iPhone and iPad.
  • Clutch saves: land a hit as the hit counter starts falling apart to save your streak for bonus points.
  • The results screen explains the grade and how many points you need for the next one.
  • Install the game to your home screen; optional reminders to come back after a long break.
  • Speed now pays: every second under 10 minutes is worth 40 points.
  • Sign in with email or Google, choose your player name, and switch between accounts on one device.
  • New lettering across the game, and many menu and results screen refinements.
