Getting it running

Ratel Rage runs in the browser. Here is how to install it, keep your progress, and fix the common problems.

Install it

Android (Chrome): open ratelrage.com/play, then on the main menu choose INSTALL GAME, or use Chrome's menu › Install app. It then opens from your home screen, full screen and sideways.

iPhone and iPad (Safari): open the game, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. (The main menu's INSTALL GAME shows the same steps.)

Computer (Chrome or Edge): use the install icon at the end of the address bar, or INSTALL GAME on the main menu.

Before you play on a phone

  • Turn the phone sideways. The game plays in landscape.
  • The first visit downloads the streets, which is a few hundred megabytes. Wi-Fi is best the first time; later visits reuse most of it.
  • Your first tap goes full screen on Android.

No sound?

Browsers only allow sound after you touch the page. Tap the screen or press a key once. Check your phone isn't on silent, and that MASTER VOLUME isn't down in Options › Audio. On a computer, M mutes and unmutes during a fight.

Your progress

Progress, best scores, settings and your touch layout are kept in this browser on this device. To keep them safe and use them on another device, SIGN IN from the main menu with email or Google. Clearing the browser's site data while signed out removes your progress.

"The streets did not load"

A file didn't arrive, usually on a weak connection. Press TRY AGAIN. If it keeps happening, close other tabs, move to Wi-Fi and reload. On older iPhones, closing other apps helps too.

The controls are in my way

On touch screens, every button can be moved, resized and faded: Options › Controls › Customize controls. Save up to three layouts. See How to play for every move.

Reminders

Where your browser supports it, the game can remind you to come back after a long break. Turn it off any time: Options › Gameplay › Return reminders.

Which browsers?

The game is built and tested for Chrome (Android and computer) and Safari (iPhone and iPad). Other up-to-date browsers usually work.

Adverts in the game

Some billboards on the street carry real adverts. They never pop up or interrupt play. In the pause menu, VISIT POSTS lets you look at them; Options › Gameplay › Autoplay video ads stops video adverts playing on their own when you pause. Advertise with us.
